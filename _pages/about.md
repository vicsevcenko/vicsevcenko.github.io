I am an Assistant Professor of Strategy at INSEAD. My research examines how firms and workers manage human capital — how talent is acquired, developed, and deployed — and how these choices shape firm performance and worker outcomes. I am particularly interested in questions at the intersection of strategy and gender.

This site houses my research, teaching, and other writing. For a current CV, see the link below. The best way to reach me is by email.
